Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

SecureCam is a service for viewing your own IP cameras in a web browser, with detection of people, vehicles and animals in the video. This policy explains what the service at this address collects, what it is used for, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it.

Summary

  • We collect what is needed to run your account and show you your cameras, and nothing for advertising.
  • Your cameras, footage, detections and alerts are visible only to your account.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers, and the site has no tracking or analytics scripts.

What we collect

Account information

  • Email address, used to sign you in and to send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password).
  • Password, stored only as a salted one-way hash (scrypt). We cannot read it.
  • Google sign-in, if you choose it: Google tells us your email address, whether Google has verified it, and an identifier for your Google account. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your contacts, files or any other Google data.

Camera settings

The names, network addresses and login details of the cameras you add, and the buildings and rooms you place them in. The camera login is stored on the server so it can connect to the camera for you. It is never sent back to your browser.

Video and detections

  • Live video from your cameras passes through the server so it can be shown in your browser. Only the last few seconds of each stream are held, and they are overwritten continuously.
  • Detections. The server looks at frames from your cameras and records sightings of people, vehicles, cats and dogs: the time, the camera, where in the picture the object was, and a still image of that moment.
  • Recognition data. To tell whether the same person or vehicle has been seen before, the server stores a numerical description of each one (for a person, of their face). This is biometric information in some places. It is used only to group sightings within your own account, is never compared across accounts, and is never sent to your browser or to anyone else.
  • Alert clips. A short video clip is saved with each alert.
  • Names you give to people, vehicles and animals.

All of this processing happens on the SecureCam server. Video and images are not sent to any outside artificial-intelligence or analytics service.

Technical information

  • One cookie, which keeps you signed in. It lasts up to 12 hours and is not used for tracking.
  • Your network address, held in memory for a short time to limit repeated failed sign-ins, and recorded in ordinary server logs.
  • Your browser may store display preferences (such as the grid layout) on your own device.

People who appear on your cameras

Your cameras may record people other than you. You are responsible for using your cameras lawfully where you live: for example pointing them only at places you are entitled to monitor, putting up notices where the law requires them, and getting consent where the law requires it for recording or for recognising faces. If you appear in footage from someone else's SecureCam account and want it removed, contact us and we will pass your request to that account holder.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show you your cameras, detections and alerts.
  • To send the account emails described above.
  • To keep the service running and to investigate faults or abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use your video or images to train detection models.

Who else handles it

  • DigitalOcean hosts the server that stores the information above.
  • Resend delivers account emails, and so receives your email address and the content of those emails.
  • Google, only if you use Google sign-in, as described above.

We do not sell personal information. We disclose it to others only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Live video: a few seconds.
  • Alerts and their clips: alerts are removed about 7 days after they were last active. Clips are bundled into daily archives that are deleted after 30 days.
  • Sightings, still images, recognition data and names: until you delete the person, vehicle or animal on the Detection page, or your account is deleted.
  • Camera settings: until you delete the camera or your account is deleted.
  • Account information: until your account is deleted.

Your choices

  • You can delete a camera at any time, and delete any detected person, vehicle or animal together with its sightings, images and clips.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, for a correction, or for your account and everything in it to be deleted, by contacting us. We will act on a deletion request within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. Contact us to use them.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and each account's data is kept separate from every other account's. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you.

Children

SecureCam is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly open accounts for them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the change is significant, we will tell account holders by email.

Contact

For any question or request about your information, contact the operator of this SecureCam server.
